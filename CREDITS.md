# Credits

The pixel art uses two packs by Kenney, all released under CC0 1.0 (public
domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

| Pack | Author | Source | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny Town | Kenney (www.kenney.nl) | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town | CC0 1.0 | Grass and dirt-road floor tiles, trees, mushrooms (`assets/pixel/tiny-town.png`) |
| Tiny Battle | Kenney (www.kenney.nl) | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-battle | CC0 1.0 | Water floor tile, flags, the palette the soldiers are drawn in (`assets/pixel/tiny-battle.png`) |

The files in `assets/pixel/` are the packs' unmodified `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png`
sheets (16 x 16 tiles, no spacing).

Everything else is drawn in code (`src/pixel/`): the soldiers (the player's
crowd seen from behind, enemies from the front, in Kenney's palette), the boss,
saws, boulders, coins, pickups, smaller far-away trees, the sky, gates, the
bitmap fonts and the HUD. No art from commercial games.
